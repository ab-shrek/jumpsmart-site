Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

JumpSmart is published by TerribleToe Productions. This policy describes data we collect when you play JumpSmart on iOS.

What we collect

  • Username you choose, and a Unity Gaming Services player id. Names are unique and shown to opponents and on public rankings.
  • Gameplay data such as match results, skill rating, and win–loss–draw record, used for online play and the leaderboard.
  • Purchase history for the optional one-time Remove Ads product, provided through Apple In-App Purchase. We do not see your payment card.
  • Device and advertising data used by LevelPlay (Unity Grow) to show optional after-match interstitial ads, unless you have purchased Remove Ads.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, or precise location through the app.

How we use it

Data is used to run online matches, keep usernames unique, show rankings, remember Remove Ads, and serve ads. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for cross-app tracking.

Online play uses Unity Gaming Services. Ads use Unity / LevelPlay.

Accounts

JumpSmart signs you in anonymously per device. Reinstalling the app starts a new player. To request deletion of your username or associated ranking data, email abhishekrocketeer@gmail.com from a message that includes your in-game name.

Children

JumpSmart is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
